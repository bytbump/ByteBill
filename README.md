# ByteBill by Byte Bump — Billing & Inventory (Windows)

## Pricing
~~₹599/-~~ **₹299/- per device** (offer price as per the new circulation; actual price ₹599/-).

## Install (client)
1. Run **Setup-ByteBill-2.30.1.exe** → Next → Install (no admin needed).
2. Open ByteBill from the Desktop icon.
3. First launch opens the **Licence** tab — copy your Product key and send it
   to your vendor, or tap **WhatsApp 7063008160** to message the administrator
   with your key pre-filled.
4. Receive your `Licence.json` file → **Browse…** → **Activate**
   (internet is needed once to confirm).
5. Settings → fill business details. Done.

## Activation (Licence)
ByteBill needs a one-time device licence (₹299/- per device, offer price;
actual ₹599/-):
- The app shows your **Product key** (this device's hardware ID). Copy it, or
  press the WhatsApp button to contact the administrator — 7063008160 — with
  the key pre-filled, to activate or get a subscription.
- Your vendor issues a `Licence.json` file for your key.
- In the Licence tab: **Browse…** to the file → **Activate**. The app confirms
  the licence online once; after that it works offline (re-check at most every
  5 minutes, with a 30-day offline grace).
- Vendors can deactivate a licence remotely — to reactivate or renew, contact
  the administrator on WhatsApp 7063008160.

## Uninstall
Windows Settings → Apps → ByteBill → Uninstall (your bills & data are kept).
